What Is Nikolaustag?

Where fun meets Gemütlichkeit

St. Nikolaus Day is a beloved German holiday that kicks off the Advent season. On the night before December 6th, children leave their shoes or boots by the door or the fireplace, and St. Nikolaus fills them with small gifts and treats.

Hosted by the Gulf Coast Enzianer Schuhplattler Verein and the German-American Society, our celebration shares this tradition with the whole community.
