Willkommen!

Eight reasons the Bay keeps coming back.

German Beer

Imported on tap, poured properly.

Traditional Food

Fresh-cooked food including Brats, Schnitzel, Sauerkraut, and Potato Pancakes.

Live Music

Oompah bands on the stage every session.

Folk Dancing

The Gulf Coast Enzianer Schuhplattler Verein perform twice each night.

Stein Contests

Hold a full liter out straight. Strongest arm wins.

Keg Tapping

The ceremonial tapping that opens the festival.

Parade of Flags

The traditional procession and national anthems at 6:00.

Kinder Corner

Games and space for the little ones.
